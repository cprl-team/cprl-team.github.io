Causal Perception and Reasoning

Causal AI research group · Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

We aim to move beyond correlation-based perception toward genuine causal reasoning, developing models that explain not only what happens but why it happens. Our research spans causal video understanding, document intelligence, healthcare, physics-informed modeling, and neuro-symbolic AI.

Research areas

Causal Video Understanding

Grounding video into events, then reasoning about the triggers that connect them.

Causal Document Intelligence

Treating document layout and structure as a causal graph over text, tables, and figures.

Causal AI in Healthcare

ICU outcome prediction on MIMIC: leakage-free prediction, explanation, and counterfactuals.

Physics-Informed Causal AI

Mechanistic models of physical processes, from prediction to intervention and digital twins.

Causal LLMs & Neuro-Symbolic AI

Do models infer cause and effect, or recall it? Pairing LLMs with symbolic reasoning.
