I lead the Causal Perception and Reasoning (CPR) group, where we work to move perception systems beyond recognizing what happens toward reasoning about why it happens. My research centers on affective computing, multimodal learning, and the analysis of physiological signals.

contact@cpr.ai.vn cpr.ai.vn Publications Team

About

Over the past several years my work has spanned facial expression and action-unit analysis, audio-visual emotion understanding, and engagement and pain estimation from physiological signals. More recently, I have focused on causal and information-theoretic approaches to these problems, asking not only whether a model predicts well but whether it captures the mechanism behind the signal.

Together with my collaborators, I have placed first in several international affective-computing challenges, including the Emotion Recognition in the Wild (EmotiW), Evoked Expressions from Videos (EEV), and EMOPAIN challenges.
