Group Lead, Causal Perception and Reasoning · Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

I hold a PhD in Artificial Intelligence Convergence from Chonnam National University, South Korea, and a BEng from Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT). I lead the Causal Perception and Reasoning (CPR) group, where we work to move perception systems from recognizing what happens toward reasoning about why it happens. My research spans affective computing, multimodal learning, and physiological-signal analysis, increasingly through a causal and information-theoretic lens.
